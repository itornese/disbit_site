Sound familiar?

Time is almost always lost in the same places.

Your client asks for a status update, and you have to piece it together every time.
To find out where a task stands, you have to ask three people.

How I work

The DISBIT path

Four phases, each with a concrete output. Technology comes last: only after understanding, designing and simplifying. And you can stop after any phase, deciding with the numbers in hand.

Discover

Process assessment

Analyze needs, existing (as-is) processes and the opportunities offered by emerging technologies.

You getAn as-is process map, identified inefficiencies, technology opportunities and an estimate of the value that can be unlocked.

Invent

Design and business case

Design tailored, innovative solutions, complete with architecture, business case and return on investment (ROI) analysis.

You getSolution architecture, delivery plan, business case with ROI and funding options.

Simplify

Process re-engineering

Simplify workflows and operating procedures by removing redundant steps, so the organization adopts the new way of working without friction.

You getRedesigned workflows, clear rules, defined roles and responsibilities, and an organization ready to adopt them.

Build

Program management

Direct and manage complex transformation programs, making sure time, budget and quality standards are met.

You getA solution delivered and adopted, people trained, results measured against agreed KPIs.

IT

Technology, last

Information Technology, but also "it": what you finally build. The most solid part.

You getTechnology chosen to fit the process, integrated and ready to evolve.

About

Ilario Tornese

Industrial Engineer · Innovation Manager

For over fifteen years I have worked where business meets technology, and today I lead transformation initiatives: I turn management objectives into processes, systems and measurable results.

In complex projects I am the link between management, business stakeholders and technical teams. I direct the whole path, from analysis to delivery: I clarify objectives and responsibilities, manage priorities, dependencies and risks, and check that every phase delivers the expected value, measured against indicators agreed with the client.

Why DISBIT

DISBIT grew out of my experience in managing complexity, where business, organization and technology must converge. It brings together the ability to synthesize, the habit of spotting the critical path early, the meticulous way I work, a method refined over years of practice and one rule I never compromise on: quality work.

The logo tells the same story. The cuts run through D, I, S and B, the phases where you analyze, design and simplify. IT stays whole: technology comes last, and it is the solid part.

I have led digitalization, automation and data analytics programs in industry, logistics, energy, services and public administration, in Italy and abroad. I work as an independent consultant and, when a project calls for it, I team up with selected technology partners while remaining your single point of contact, from analysis to results. I also assess whether a project may qualify for grants, tax credits or subsidized financing.

What innovation is

A new or changed entity, realizing or redistributing value.

The definition in ISO 56000, the basis of innovation management and of my Innovation Manager certification (UNI 11814).

That’s why, in a DISBIT project, technology is never the goal. What counts is the value it creates: time given back to people, errors avoided, decisions based on reliable data.
