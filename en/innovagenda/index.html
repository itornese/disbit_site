INNOVAGENDA

12 principles for managing innovation

by Ilario Tornese · Certified Innovation Manager (UNI 11814)

A new or changed entity, realizing or redistributing value.

The definition in ISO 56000, the basis of innovation management and of my Innovation Manager certification (UNI 11814).

12 principles for the strategic management of innovation, inspired by the ISO 56000 series. Not a checklist, but the management lens the DISBIT method applies to processes.
